# Pricing of the researched social media tools, 2026-11-18

Every figure comes from the six research reports of 2026-11-18, which read each vendor's own pricing page on that date. Where a figure comes from somewhere else, the row says so.
- **UNVERIFIED** means the reports could not confirm it.
- **Prices are regular list prices in USD.** Black Friday offers were live at SocialBee, SocialPilot, MeetEdgar and Predis on that day and are left out; for Predis the regular price could not be confirmed.
- **Taxes:** prices exclude tax unless stated.
- **Nextdoor** is not in the table: it is a network, not a management tool, and sells no plans to manage it.

## What the table shows (facts only, no recommendation)

- **What they charge by:**
  - **User:** Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse.
  - **Connected account:** Buffer, Publer.
  - **Brand:** Metricool.
  - **Workspace:** Planable.
  - **Tiers bundling accounts, users and workspaces:** everyone else.
- **Cheapest paid entry, monthly billing:**
  - per account: Publer $5 and Buffer $6;
  - Missinglettr $15;
  - Lately and FeedHive $19;
  - Later and Metricool $25;
  - SocialBee, ContentStudio and Ocoya $29;
  - MeetEdgar $29.99;
  - SocialPilot $30;
  - Sendible $35;
  - Planable $39;
  - Loomly $65;
  - Statusbrew $89;
  - Sprout Social, Agorapulse and Vista Social $99, and Hootsuite $99 billed annually.
- **PayPal:**
  - Confirmed accepted at seven: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse, Metricool, Publer, SocialBee, SocialPilot.
  - Stated not accepted at Buffer, Later and Vista Social (cards only).
  - Not listed at Sendible, MeetEdgar, Statusbrew, Planable and Loomly.
  - Unconfirmed for the six AI tools.
- **Trials:**
  - 30 days at Sprout Social, Agorapulse and MeetEdgar.
  - 14 days at most others.
  - 7 days at ContentStudio, FeedHive, Ocoya and Publer's Professional plan.
  - 3 days at Predis.
  - None as standard at Metricool.
- **Free plans:** Buffer, Publer, Metricool, Later, Planable and Loomly have one; Agorapulse's is now AI-only.
- **X often costs extra:**
  - Metricool $10 to $12 per account.
  - Vista Social $29 per profile.
  - Agorapulse X Plus $69 per profile.
- **Annual discounts:**
  - lowest, about 15%: SocialPilot and Sendible;
  - most, 20% to 25%: for example Sprout Social, Agorapulse, Later, Loomly;
  - highest, about a third: ContentStudio's Standard plan ($19 against $29) and FeedHive's Business plan ($69 against $99).
